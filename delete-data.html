Delete your Cat Zip data

Cat Zip · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Cat Zip has no sign-up and does not ask for your real name, email address or phone number. Most of your game data is kept only on your phone. Only if you use Teams is some information stored on our server (Google Firebase). This page explains how to delete it.

How to request deletion

  • Step 1 (optional): in the game, open your team and tap Leave team. This removes you from the team right away, together with your points in the current challenge.
  • Step 2: send an email to catzipgame@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Cat Zip data". You can also do this from the game: Settings → Support.
  • In the email, write your player name and the name or code of your team (or the last team you were in), so we can find your anonymous player record.

We delete your data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your anonymous player ID record;
  • your player name, avatar and frame as shown to your team, and your Team Champion titles;
  • your team membership and all your team board results (points, times, hints);
  • if you are the last member of a team, the team itself (name, emblem and code).

What is kept

We do not keep any of the Teams information listed above after deletion, and we keep no extra backup copies. If you were the captain, another member becomes captain and the team continues for the other players.

Data on your phone and ad data

  • Your progress, hints and settings are stored only on your phone. You can delete them at any time by uninstalling Cat Zip or clearing its data in your phone's settings.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See our Privacy Policy for details.
